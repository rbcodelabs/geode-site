## Features

- Adds draggable center-pane dividers with proportional sizing that persists across restarts.
- Lets integrations request an initial split ratio, enabling Claude Threads' conversation-first workspace to open at 30/70 while ordinary Geode splits remain 50/50.
- Adds keyboard resizing, separator semantics, current/minimum/maximum accessibility values, focus styling, and a larger transparent pointer target.

## Improvements

- Safely normalizes missing, malformed, or non-positive persisted sizes.
- Preserves unrelated pane shares when panes are inserted or removed.
- Cleans up active resizing on pointer up/cancel, lost capture, window blur, subsequent drags, and workspace disposal.
- Keeps mobile behavior unchanged without rewriting desktop proportions.
