## What’s New in v0.5.11

### Bug Fixes

- **Responsive document tabs** — tabs now shrink down to their icons when window space is tight instead of enforcing a wide minimum or disappearing into overflow. All open tabs remain reachable.
- **Consistent sidebar controls** — sidebar tab icons stay compact, and both collapse buttons now match the size, icon scale, spacing, and vertical alignment of their neighboring sidebar tabs.
- **Working tab close buttons** — clicking the X on a document tab now closes it reliably; keyboard shortcuts continue to work as before.

### Quality

- Added Electron regression coverage for roomy and crowded tab layouts, compact sidebar controls, collapse-button alignment, and click-to-close behavior.
- Verified with 694 unit tests and 35 Electron end-to-end tests.

This patch includes [PR #63](https://github.com/rbcodelabs/geode/pull/63).
