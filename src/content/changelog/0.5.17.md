## What's New in v0.5.17

### Features
- **Plugin action ribbon** — Geode now has a persistent left ribbon for plugin-contributed actions, including accessible controls, a Settings entry point, visibility persistence, and correct cleanup when plugins unload. ([#66](https://github.com/rbcodelabs/geode/pull/66))

### Bug Fixes
- **Dismissible notices** — Plugin notices and internal notifications now have an accessible close button and safe click-to-dismiss behavior. Timed notices still expire automatically, persistent notices remain until dismissed, and interactive controls inside notices continue to work. ([#68](https://github.com/rbcodelabs/geode/pull/68))

### Notes
- macOS installers remain ad-hoc signed and are not notarized; the existing first-launch Gatekeeper instructions still apply.
- Includes Apple Silicon and Intel DMG/ZIP installers plus automatic-update metadata.
