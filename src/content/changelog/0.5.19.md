## What’s New in v0.5.19

### Pinned tabs

- Right-click any main-area tab and choose **Pin** or **Unpin**.
- Pinned tabs stay open when you follow internal links, making persistent views such as Kanban boards and Claude Threads safe to keep in place.
- Pin state and the visual pin indicator update immediately and survive app relaunches.
- Hosted plugins can control the same behavior through Obsidian-compatible `WorkspaceLeaf.setPinned()` and `togglePinned()` APIs.

### Compatibility and quality

- Adds an Electron regression covering the context menu, protected navigation, persistence, plugin APIs, and unpinning.
- No migration or configuration changes are required.
