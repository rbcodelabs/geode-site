## Highlights

- **Plugin keymap scope stack** — Geode now exposes live `App.scope` and `App.keymap` contracts, dispatches logical key events through the active plugin scope chain before app commands, and gives plugin modals an exactly-once, generation-safe child scope lifecycle. Real terminal-plugin coverage verifies focus capture and restoration. (#323)
- **Stronger View and WorkspaceLeaf compatibility evidence** — a real CommonJS plugin fixture now certifies construction and identity, async lifecycle ordering, persistent and ephemeral state, display text, exact rendered icon identity, view state, and pinning behavior in Electron. Conservative gaps remain explicitly partial in the parity ledger. (#324)

## Compatibility and quality

- The parity ledger was regenerated after merging both overlapping evidence sets and remains current at 2,856 requirements.
- `KeymapContext` now exposes the documented runtime `key`, exact platform `modifiers`, and populated `vkey`; broader legacy/layout/special-key interpretation remains explicitly partial.
- Modal cleanup is safe across synchronous failures, rejected async opens, explicit close, and close/reopen races.
- Audio Recorder's existing partial evidence remains preserved through the ledger regeneration.

## Validation

- Type-check, build, focused unit tests, focused real-Electron tests, mobile CI, desktop CI, and CodeQL passed on the merged changes.
- The release workflow packages an Apple Silicon DMG and ZIP signed with Developer ID, notarized, stapled, and accompanied by the updater manifest, blockmaps, and pinned publisher metadata.

## Notes

- This is a macOS Apple Silicon early-alpha release. Windows, Linux, and Intel Mac builds are not included.
- No migration steps are required.
