## What's New in v0.5.18

### Improvements
- **Native wake lock for hosted agent work** — Geode now exposes a secure, token-based bridge to Electron's native power-save blocker. Compatible hosted plugins such as Claude Threads can keep long-running work active without launching a separate operating-system process. Tokens are isolated to their renderer and automatically cleaned up when the window closes. ([#73](https://github.com/rbcodelabs/geode/pull/73))

### Notes
- macOS installers remain ad-hoc signed and are not notarized; the existing first-launch Gatekeeper instructions still apply.
- Includes Apple Silicon and Intel DMG/ZIP installers plus automatic-update metadata.
